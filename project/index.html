عقل نبضNABD's brain

شبكة عصبية درّبناها بنفسناA neural network we trained ourselves

تعلّمت من تسجيلات 175 شخص كيف يظهر النبض في إشارة الرادار، وصارت تقرأه عند ناس ما شافتهم من قبل. From the recordings of 175 people, it learned how a heartbeat shows up in a radar signal, and now reads it in people it has never seen.

Transformer layers compare the parts of the signal with each other (the yellow lines) and use what they learned to tell the real heartbeat from the noise.
